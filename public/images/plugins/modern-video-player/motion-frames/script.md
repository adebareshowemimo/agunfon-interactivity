## 1. The hook (0:00–0:10) — the 5–10s grab

> **"You hit upload. And that's the last thing you really know about that video. Who watched it? Did they skip to the end? Did it leak? Moodle just… plays it."**

Cold-open on the loss of control every trainer/admin feels: you publish a video and go blind. Three fast stabs — *watched? skipped? leaked?* — set up the product's three pillars (track / protect / prove).

## 2. Full voiceover narration (source of truth = `voiceover.txt`)

> You hit upload. And that's the last thing you really know about that video. Who watched it? Did they skip to the end? Did it leak? Moodle just… plays it.
>
> Modern Video Player changes that. It turns any Moodle video into an activity you can track, protect, and prove.
>
> **Track it.** Now you see exactly how every learner watches. An engagement heatmap. A retention curve that reveals where they drop off. The parts they rewatch. All on one dashboard, across every course and cohort.
>
> **Protect it.** Stamp every stream with the learner's own name. Serve it through signed URLs from your own S3 or Azure storage. Deter screen capture with a tamper-evident audit trail.
>
> **Prove it.** Full audit exports. xAPI to your LRS. LTI 1.3 with grade passback. Play from an upload, YouTube, Vimeo, or your own cloud, in seven player skins.
>
> **[Standard ending]** Modern Video Player. Know exactly how your videos are watched. One-time licence, a full year of support, no subscription. Start free on the Moodle Plugins Directory, or go Premium on the Moodle Marketplace. **Modern Video Player makes Moodle work harder.**

**AIDA:** Attention (blind-after-upload hook) → Interest (an activity you track, protect, prove) → Desire (the three pillars, real features) → Action (open-core start-free → Premium on the Marketplace). **No em-dashes** — short sentences and commas carry the pacing; the single ellipsis in the hook is the only deliberate pause.
