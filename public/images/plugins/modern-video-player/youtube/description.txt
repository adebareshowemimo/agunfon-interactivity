You upload a video to Moodle and go blind. Modern Video Player changes that: it turns any Moodle video into an activity you can track, protect, and prove.

▶ WHAT PREMIUM DOES
• See how every learner watches — engagement heatmap, audience-retention curve, and rewatch intensity, on a cohort dashboard across your courses
• Protect your content — a dynamic per-learner watermark (name & email), signed URLs from your own S3 or Azure storage, and screen-capture deterrence with a tamper-evident audit log
• Prove completion — full audit exports (CSV, JSON, Excel, ODS), xAPI/cmi5 to your LRS, and LTI 1.3 publishing with grade passback
• Play from an upload, direct URL, YouTube, Vimeo, or your own cloud — in seven player skins (Classic, YouTube, Vimeo, Netflix, Plyr, Apple, Minimal)
• Server-validated watched segments, seek & playback-rate enforcement, and suspicious-activity flags

✅ OPEN-CORE
The free Community edition is a complete, GPL video activity (player, captions, transcript, chapters, bookmarks, Focus Mode, validated tracking, completion & gradebook). Premium adds the analytics, protection, and integrations above as an in-place upgrade that keeps all your existing data.
