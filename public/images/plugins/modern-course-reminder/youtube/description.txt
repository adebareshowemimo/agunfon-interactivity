Half your learners never finish. Modern Course Reminder fixes that — automatically. It's a rule-based Moodle plugin that chases incomplete, inactive, overdue and not-started learners, and escalates to their manager.

▶ WHAT IT DOES
• Set one rule and it reminds learners automatically — never started, inactive for weeks, past the deadline, or still incomplete
• Target a single course, a whole category, or a cohort — down to specific activities
• Sends by email and Moodle notification, on your schedule, in your branding
• Escalates still-incomplete learners to their line manager (custom profile field, manual mapping table, or Moodle manager role)
• Draft reminder templates with Moodle's built-in AI, then preview them with sample data
• Every reminder is queued, de-duplicated, retried and logged — no spam, nothing slips through
• Scheduled campaigns, manager digests, delivery logs, effectiveness analytics and a Report Builder datasource

✅ WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
Core Moodle and the free reminder plugin send one basic nudge. Modern Course Reminder is a full rule, escalation and analytics engine — so your courses actually get finished.
