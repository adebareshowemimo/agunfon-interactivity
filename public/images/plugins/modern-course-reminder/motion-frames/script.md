# Modern Course Reminder — Promo Video Script (v1 for review)

**Plugin:** `local_moderncoursereminder` · Moodle 4.5–5.2 · STABLE v1.0.3
**Target runtime:** ~72–80s (155 words @ ~145 wpm + pause cues) — inside your 60–90s brief
**Format:** motion graphics + real screen recordings/screenshots
**Hook angle:** low completion / drop-off · **Close:** value-framed (one-time license + 1 year support included, no subscription) · **CTA:** Moodle Plugins Directory
**License model (ThemeForest/Envato-style):** one-time "regular license" purchase, **1 year of free support included**. Silent on support renewal for now (no renewal price stated). Reference tone: Landrick on ThemeForest ("Regular License" + support-included framing). No "lifetime updates" claim — not confirmed.
**Cardinal rule check:** every spoken claim traces to a real feature (rule builder, triggers, scope, channels, AI drafting, escalation, queue/dedupe/retry/log, analytics, privacy). Nothing invented.

---

## 1. The hook (0:00–0:10) — the 5–10s attention grab

> **"You enrolled five hundred learners. Six weeks later, half of them never finished — not because your course was bad, but because nobody reminded them."**

Cold-open on a single dramatic number that every Moodle admin and trainer feels in their gut. No logo, no product yet — just the pain. The reveal ("nobody reminded them") sets up the product as the obvious fix.

---

## 2. Full voiceover narration (source of truth = `voiceover.txt`)

> You enrolled five hundred learners. Six weeks later, half of them never finished — not because your course was bad, but because nobody reminded them.
>
> Modern Course Reminder fixes that — automatically. Set one rule, and it chases them for you. Forever.
>
> It finds the learners who slipped — never started, inactive for weeks, past the deadline, still incomplete. Target one course, a whole category, or a cohort. Then it nudges them by email and Moodle notification, on your schedule, in your branding.
>
> Need the wording fast? Let Moodle's built-in AI draft the template. Still no response? It escalates straight to their manager.
>
> Every reminder is queued, deduplicated, retried, and logged — so no one gets spammed, and nothing slips through. And a live dashboard shows completion climbing.
>
> Built for Moodle 4.5 to 5.2. One-time license — buy it once, with a full year of support included. No subscription.
>
> Modern Course Reminder. Turn enrolments into completions. Find it now on the Moodle Plugins Directory.

**AIDA structure:** Attention (hook) → Interest ("fixes that automatically, set one rule") → Desire (the real capability rattle) → Action (one-time license + Plugins Directory CTA). Em-dashes and "?" double as TTS pause cues.
