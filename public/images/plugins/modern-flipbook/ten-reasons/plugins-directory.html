Modern Flipbook

Modern Flipbook turns an ordinary PDF into a controlled, Moodle-native learning activity. Upload a document, present it as a polished flipbook reader, and let Moodle record who actually opened, read, and acknowledged it.

On that foundation it adds real completion rules, a compliance acknowledgement experience, per-page analytics with drop-off detection, gradebook integration, watermark accountability, and full backup, restore and Privacy API support — all GPLv3, with bundled PDF.js and StPageFlip and no external service.
