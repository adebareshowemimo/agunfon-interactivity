# Modern Learner Dashboard — Promo Video Script (v1 for review)

**Plugin:** `block_modernlearnerdashboard` · Moodle 4.5–5.2 · STABLE v1.4.17 · GPLv3
**Target runtime:** ~78–85s (~184 words @ ~145 wpm + hook pauses) — inside the 60–90s brief
**Format:** motion graphics + real screen captures (extracted from the edited reasons deck)
**Hook angle:** fragmentation / drop-off — "everything is on a different page, so learners give up"
**Close:** value-framed (one-time licence + 1 year of support included, no subscription) · **CTA:** Moodle Marketplace (premium plugin, `marketplace.moodle.com/plugins/73`)
**License model (ThemeForest/Envato-style):** one-time purchase, 1 year of support included. Silent on support renewal. No "lifetime updates" claim.
**Cardinal-rule check:** every spoken claim traces to a real feature — one-block overview, Continue learning + resume, Course Library + self-enrol, Calendar + due-soon, badges/certificates, learning transcript, inline profile editing, core-only architecture (no service/tables/cron), null privacy provider, lazy-load + cache, theme compatibility. Nothing invented.

---

## 1. The hook (0:00–0:11) — the 5–10s attention grab

> **"Your learners log in to Moodle... then what? Their courses, grades, deadlines, badges, and profile are scattered across five different pages. So they stop looking, and they stop coming back."**

Cold-open on the pain every Moodle admin knows: the default learner experience is *fragmented*. No logo, no product yet — just five things flying to five corners of the screen and a learner giving up. The single ellipsis after "Moodle" is the one dramatic beat. Sets up the product as the obvious fix: pull it all back into one place.

## 2. Full voiceover narration (source of truth = `voiceover.txt`)

> Your learners log in to Moodle... then what? Their courses, grades, deadlines, badges, and profile are scattered across five different pages. So they stop looking, and they stop coming back.
>
> Modern Learner Dashboard gives every learner one home. Courses, completion, due dates, grades, badges, and certificates, together in a single block on the Dashboard.
>
> Continue learning surfaces the courses they opened last, with progress bars and a one-click resume. A searchable course library lets them find and self-enrol in what's next. A built-in calendar and a due-soon count keep deadlines from slipping. Badges and certificates sit front and centre, and a learning transcript records every completed course, ready for audit.
>
> Learners even edit their own profile right here, so your team stops fielding profile tickets.
>
> And it runs on core Moodle. No external service, no new tables, no cron. Privacy-clean, fast on big sites, and native in the theme you already run.
>
> Modern Learner Dashboard. Bring every learner home. One-time licence, a full year of support, no subscription. Get it on the Moodle Marketplace. Modern Learner Dashboard: everything your learners are working toward, in one place.

**AIDA structure:** Attention (fragmentation hook) → Interest ("one home… a single block") → Desire (the real capability rattle) → Action (one-time licence + Marketplace CTA). No em-dashes in the read — short sentences and commas carry the rhythm; one ellipsis in the hook.
